## 2. Hypotheses: importance and admissibility

| Hypothesis | Importance | Status | Admissible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| H0 flood skill = rain x exposure, ranking inside an event | core claim | supported, robust to trigger / tropical window / CV (st. 8, 36) | Yes |
| Claim history adds beyond rain + exposure | high | +0.026, learners converge near 0.81 (st. 19, 20) | Yes |
| H1 footprint x exposure needs 2-D reading | high | CNN gain at 4 km, but exposure channels not isolated; "dropping exposure channels does not hurt" (st. 46) | Partly: the 2-D gain is a local rain read, H1 itself not supported |
| H3 position tolerance / regional bias | medium | 4 km gain is position-specific; 0.25 deg CNN ties boosting (st. 42) | Not supported at forecast resolution |
| H4 learning stage (observed-trained, applied to members, E4) | highest remaining | untested; needs downscaled / finer forecast | Open, the only route to forecast-mode gain |
| H2 flow-aligned structure (E3, ERA5 ceiling) | low | ERA5 -0.011 borderline worse (st. 51) | Effectively closed |
| "CNN is data-limited, more states help" | medium | supported only in FL (+0.029); LA worse; TX flat (st. 49) | Weak, state-dependent |
| "Embedding is one shared decision space" | low | rain direction shared (cos 0.90-0.98), state recoverable at 0.90 (st. 50) | Only for the rain direction |
| "CNN reads long-range / contrast structure" | low | corrected: reach is 2-3 px (st. 47 corrects st. 46) | Rejected, keep the correction |
| "Boosting is feature-limited, not data-limited" | medium | pooling does not lift it (st. 45) | Admissible |
| Label cleaning improves the target | low | never beats original (st. 23) | Rejected |

Admissibility cautions to carry forward:
- CNN vs boosting compares a 3-5 seed mean with a single boosting model (st. 42); a boosting ensemble was never run.
- Seed spread (0.003-0.017 top-5%) is as large as several reported differences in stages 46-52; 2 seeds per variant.
- "Boosting on the CNN's own embedding" and "boosting with a hand-built smoothed feature" are untested, so "the CNN is better" is a statement about these two pipelines, not a mechanism.
- The stage 49 held-out-state comparisons were not tested for significance.
- Block-group metrics condition on days with at least one claim (stages 45, 49); all-days results exist only for stages 38-40.
